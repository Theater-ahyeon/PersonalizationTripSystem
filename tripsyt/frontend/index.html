旅游推荐

GET/api/areas
获取景区列表 ?sort=heat|rating&topK=10&keyword=xxx&category=xxx
GET/api/areas/:id
获取景区详情
GET/api/buildings
获取景区建筑物 ?areaId=x
GET/api/recommend
推荐景区 ?sort=heat|rating|interest&topK=10

路线规划

POST/api/route/shortest
最短距离路径 {start, end, areaId}
POST/api/route/fastest
最短时间路径 {start, end, areaId}
POST/api/route/multipoint
途经多点路径 {start, waypoints[], areaId}
POST/api/route/transport
指定交通工具路径 {start, end, transport, areaId}
POST/api/route/indoor
室内导航 {buildingId, start, end}

场所查询

GET/api/facilities
查找附近设施 ?areaId=x&nodeId=x&range=x&category=xxx

旅游日记

GET/api/diaries
获取日记列表 ?sort=heat|rating&topK=10
POST/api/diaries
创建日记 {userId, title, content, destination}
GET/api/diaries/:id
获取日记详情
POST/api/diaries/:id/rate
评分 {score}
POST/api/diaries/:id/view
浏览

日记交流

GET/api/diaries/search
搜索日记 ?destination=xxx / ?title=xxx / ?keyword=xxx
POST/api/diaries/:id/compress
压缩日记
POST/api/diaries/:id/decompress
解压日记

美食推荐

GET/api/foods
获取美食列表 ?areaId=x&sort=heat|rating|distance&topK=10&cuisine=xxx&keyword=xxx
